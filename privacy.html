Privacy policy

For "Atsushi landing page" at https://anishimori123.github.io/landing-page/ · Last updated November 27, 2026

This is a personal start page made by Atsushi for his own use. It has no server, no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Google Calendar

  • If you choose Sign in with Google, Google gives your browser a short-lived pass (about 1 hour) to read and change events in your own Google Calendar.
  • The page uses it only to show your upcoming events and to add, change or delete events when you ask it to.
  • Your calendar data goes straight from Google to your browser. It is not sent to, stored on, or shared with any other server or person, and it is not used for ads.
  • The pass is kept only in your own browser and is removed when you click Sign out.
  • You can remove the page's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Tasks

  • If you also tick the Google Tasks box at sign-in, the same short-lived pass lets the page read and change the tasks in your own default Google Tasks list.
  • The page uses it only to keep the to-do box and that list the same: it adds, ticks and deletes tasks when you do so in the to-do box.
  • Your tasks go straight between Google and your browser. They are not sent to, stored on, or shared with any other server or person, and they are not used for ads.
  • If you leave the box unticked, to-dos stay only in your browser.

Weather, to-dos and settings

  • Weather and city search come from Open-Meteo. The page sends them only city coordinates or the city name you type.
  • Cities, clock choices and similar settings are saved only in your browser (localStorage) and never uploaded. To-dos are saved there too, and are uploaded only to your own Google Tasks if you allowed that (see above).
